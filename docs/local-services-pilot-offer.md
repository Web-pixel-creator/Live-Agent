# Local Services Pilot Offer

## Product

`AI Dispatcher for Local Services`

Phone-first and Telegram-ready intake for small service companies in Tashkent.
The assistant captures the request, normalizes the job card, prepares estimate
inputs, drafts customer confirmation, and hands the operator an approval-ready
dispatch payload.

## Best First Buyers

1. AC repair teams with 1-3 masters and frequent after-hours calls
2. emergency plumbers losing urgent jobs at night or on weekends
3. cleaning teams quoting manually in Telegram and phone threads
4. windows, doors, ceilings, or fit-out teams that book measurement visits

These teams are good first buyers because missed or delayed responses directly
turn into lost bookings. The measurement lane is included only when the company
books a specialist visit; stock, delivery, payment, and material substitution
remain outside the pilot.

## What The Pilot Includes

1. phone intake and Telegram fallback in one operator-reviewed flow
2. district, urgency, slot, and pricing inputs captured in a structured job card
3. photos, approximate sizes, or issue description captured when relevant
4. customer confirmation draft before any external send
5. master/operator handoff draft before any dispatch or measurement visit
6. evidence trail for transcript, media request, and approval posture
7. tracked pilot metrics for response time, bookings, and manual edits

## What The Pilot Does Not Do

1. autonomous dispatch without human approval
2. final price promises without operator review
3. payment collection
4. construction-material stock or delivery automation
5. generic multi-industry automation

## Day-One Setup

1. choose one phone line and one Telegram handoff path
2. define district coverage and escalation owner
3. define the pricing floor, ceiling, and when approval is mandatory
4. define what the assistant may confirm versus what only an operator may confirm
5. choose the one person who approves slots and handoffs during the pilot

## Success Criteria For 14 Days

1. every inbound request is captured in one job-card format
2. missed-call recovery is visible and measurable
3. first reply time drops to the agreed target
4. operators stop rewriting every handoff from scratch
5. at least one saved booking or recovered urgent job is attributable to the flow

## Pricing Posture

Use the pilot as a fixed monthly service, not a custom platform build.

Suggested starting posture:

1. small team: fixed monthly fee
2. larger team: higher fixed monthly fee after pilot proof
3. usage-based pricing only after real call volume is measured

## Pilot Story In One Sentence

`We do not replace your dispatcher. We make sure every call or Telegram request
turns into an operator-approved job card before the customer disappears.`
